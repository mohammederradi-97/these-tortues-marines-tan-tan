# Marine Turtles of Tan-Tan – Research Project

## Overview

This repository is part of a doctoral research project entitled:

**"Ecology and Conservation of Marine Turtles in the Tan-Tan Region (Central-Southern Atlantic Morocco): Analysis of Fisheries Interactions, Strandings and Potential Nesting Habitats."**

The research focuses on the ecology and conservation of marine turtles along the Atlantic coast of the Tan-Tan region, Morocco.

## Research Objectives

The project aims to:

- Document and analyze marine turtle interactions with fisheries.
- Monitor and investigate marine turtle strandings.
- Identify and assess potential nesting habitats along the coastline.
- Contribute to marine turtle conservation in southern Morocco.

## Main Research Areas

### 1. Fisheries Interactions
Documentation of incidental captures, fishing gear types, interaction locations and turtle condition.

### 2. Marine Turtle Strandings
Monitoring and analysis of stranding events, spatial distribution and potential causes of mortality.

### 3. Potential Nesting Habitats
Field surveys to investigate beaches and identify potential nesting habitats.

## Study Area

Tan-Tan region, Central-Southern Atlantic coast of Morocco.

## Data Availability

Sensitive and confidential research data are not publicly shared. They contain sensitive information (vessel identity, photos, vessel positions, nesting-site locations). Only non-sensitive documentation and authorized materials may be made available through this repository. Anonymised tables will be deposited on Zenodo with a DOI after publication of the related articles and not before the thesis defence.
