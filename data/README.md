# Research Data

This directory is dedicated to the documentation of research data.

## Data Categories

### 1. Fisheries Interactions
Marine turtle incidental captures and interactions with fishing activities.

### 2. Marine Turtle Strandings
Records of marine turtle stranding events along the Moroccan Atlantic coast.

### 3. Potential Nesting Habitats
Field survey observations of potential nesting beaches.

## Data Protection

Sensitive geographical coordinates, vessel information and personal data are not publicly shared.
